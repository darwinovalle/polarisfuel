Pathfinder Guide

How to configure your best route

Quick Start

  1. Type a valid origin and destination in the U.S.
  2. Set Time and Price weights based on your preference.
  3. Fill Vehicle MPG, Tank Capacity, and Start Fuel.
  4. Select one route option from the ranked list.
  5. Review distance, duration, fuel cost, and stop details.

Tip: start with balanced weights, then move one slider at a time to understand route behavior.

Parameter Guide

Time Weight

Higher values prioritize shorter travel time even if fuel cost rises.

Price Weight

Higher values prioritize lower estimated fuel spend and efficient stop choices.

Vehicle MPG

Use your real-world MPG. Lower MPG usually increases cost and stop count.

Tank Capacity

Bigger tank means longer range and often fewer refuel stops.

Start Fuel (%)

Initial fuel level impacts first-leg range and minimum required stops.

Tuning Playbooks

Fastest arrival

Set Time high and Price lower. Good for tight delivery windows or schedule constraints.

Lowest fuel spend

Set Price high and Time lower. Useful when fuel budget is the primary objective.

Balanced operations

Keep Time and Price near mid-range values for a practical compromise of speed and cost.

Understand The Output

  • Route Options rank from best score to worst score.
  • Distance and Duration show physical and time impact.
  • Fuel Cost estimates trip fuel expense using your assumptions.
  • Score is a weighted metric from your Time and Price sliders.
  • Route Details and map markers show the exact stop sequence.
